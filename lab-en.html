BYEOLDAM – Tarot Lab

Draw, act and revisit

Draw three of 78 cards. Record an interpretation, a counter-perspective and an action. Revisit the same cards with lived experience in your book and card map.

BYEOLDAM is a personal tarot reflection lab. Compare your own interpretations with lived experience instead of receiving generated prophecies. • Choose three cards from the full 78-card deck. Preserve their order, date and your question. • Read three lenses: your first interpretation, a counter-perspective and a small action. You write each response. • Compare real options, benefits, trade-offs, observed evidence and unknowns. Set an action, an observation criterion and a review date. Starting preserves the original interpretation and expectation. • Append observations, differences and next steps beside the same original cards. • Read records chronologically in My book with adjustable text size. My card map links appearance counts to your saved experiments. Counts and subjective confidence are not fortune or success scores. • Keep a private journal. Export a JSON backup and merge missing records without overwriting existing IDs. New experiments are free and work without an account, AI, a backend or new purchases. Records stay on device. There is no automatic cloud restoration: back up before deleting the app or changing devices. Korean, English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese are supported. Previous letters in Settings preserves historical orders and imported PDFs. This version does not sell new paid readings or a six-hour delivery service. Card symbolism offers perspectives, not factual predictions, knowledge of another person’s mind or professional advice. Do not use tarot as evidence for health, investment or other important decisions. Support: gkstmdvlf1@naver.com
