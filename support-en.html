How do I send an order?

Set up an email account in the iPhone Mail app. After a verified Apple purchase, open the order in the letterbox, choose Send order email and personally tap Send in Mail. The message includes your topic, selected cards, answers, PDF language and a JSON signed transaction attachment. Check the operator acknowledgement: a Mail send result alone does not confirm receipt. Cancelled or failed sending can be retried on the same order without another purchase.

What will the reading contain?

The first page gives a clear recommendation for your concern, followed by the three cards’ reasoning, question-specific answers, a comparison of choices, conditions that would change the recommendation and practical actions. The 78-card deck is shuffled and you choose three cards. Each of the 70 topics has its own illustration. Readings use symbolism and your input; they do not establish future facts or someone else’s thoughts.

When and in what language will the PDF arrive?

The operator creates and reviews the PDF, then emails it within 6 hours of actual receipt of your order email. Little Star Letter is 10 pages; Deep Star Story is 25 pages. Korean, English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese are supported. Choose the language in app settings before ordering and confirm it on the order review. Changing the app language later does not change an existing order’s PDF language. This is AI-assisted manual creation, not live counselling.

How do I import my PDF?

Save the attachment to the iPhone Files app, open the matching BYEOLDAM letterbox order and choose Import PDF. Keep the supplied filename, for example 별담-orderUUID.pdf. The app checks the order identifier and page count. A SAMPLE PDF cannot fulfill a paid order.

What if I reinstall or change devices?

Orders, journal entries and imported PDFs are stored on device. Automatic synchronization and restoration on another device are not supported. Keep your received email and PDF separately. For unfinished payment verification, use purchase recovery in the letterbox. Contact support with the order ID rather than buying a completed consumable order again.

Delays, duplicate orders or refunds

Send the order ID, product name and problem time to gkstmdvlf1@naver.com. Do not send passwords, card numbers or identity numbers. Check spam and outbox folders if acknowledgement or delivery is missing. Request an Apple refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple reviews and handles the request.

How can I delete my records?

Delete individual journal entries in the app. Deleting device data may be limited while payment or order processing is unfinished. Request access, correction or deletion of already emailed content through support. App deletion and deletion of operator/customer email copies are separate processes.
