2. Information and purposes
To receive, create and deliver a purchased PDF and answer support requests, we process your nickname, delivery email, adult age range, concern and question answers, selected topic/product/cards, language, consent records, order ID and Apple signed transaction verification data. Apple handles payment; the app does not receive payment card details or Apple passwords.
Free journal entries remain on your device and are not sent to the operator or AI. We do not use advertising/tracking SDKs or sell information for advertising.

3. Order email and processing in Korea
When you personally tap Send in Apple Mail, the order and a JSON attachment for signed transaction verification are sent to gkstmdvlf1@naver.com. Your chosen email provider and Naver Corporation's mail service transmit and store these messages. The operator in Korea checks the order and replies with a manually created, reviewed PDF to your chosen email address.
The app does not automatically send orders to an order server or the OpenAI API. Mail's send result does not establish actual receipt by the operator; please check the acknowledgement reply. Your email provider's privacy policy also applies.

4. AI processing and international transfer
The operator removes the nickname, email and Apple payment data, and removes or generalizes identifying names, employers, precise addresses and contacts in free text before manually entering only the adult age range, topic, cards, concern and answers into the operator's ChatGPT account. Separate consent for AI processing and international transfer is obtained in addition to consent for collecting and using order data.
Recipient: OpenAI OpCo, L.L.C., privacy@openai.com. Primary storage country: United States. Subprocessor locations may also include the UK, EEA, South Africa, Australia, Singapore, the Philippines and other locations disclosed in OpenAI's South Korea privacy addendum linked below.
Time and method: manual internet entry during creation and review after the order email is checked. Purpose: generating and reviewing the requested tarot PDF and service security. Data transferred: minimized adult age range, concern, answers, topic and selected cards. Nickname, email and signed transaction data are excluded.
The operator keeps creation conversations separate by order and deletes them within 30 days after completion. OpenAI generally deletes data within 30 days after deletion, subject to security, safety, legal retention and other stated exceptions. During processing, material is retained for creation and revision.
The operator disables model training use for new creation conversations/tasks and does not submit customer-content feedback, shared links or additional training consent. Training opt-out does not mean immediate deletion or zero retention.
You may refuse or withdraw international-transfer consent. Without consent, the paid AI PDF cannot be created or purchased; free topic browsing and the journal remain available. If consent is withdrawn after payment but before creation, contact support for processing suspension and Apple's refund route.
OpenAI policy: https://openai.com/policies/row-privacy-policy/

5. Retention and deletion
The operator deletes concern text, AI input and creation conversations, working PDFs and working email copies within 30 days after completion. Unfinished or revised orders are kept for the time needed to resolve them; long-unresolved cases are discussed with support for suspension or a refund route. You manage copies in your own email and on your device.
Minimum statutory transaction records are separated from the personal reading text: contract/cancellation and payment/supply records for 5 years; complaints/disputes for 3 years; advertising/display records for 6 months under applicable Korean requirements. The full concern is not routinely retained as a payment record. Material subject to a legal dispute or retention obligation is restricted until that obligation ends.
Electronic files and mail copies are deleted, including trash cleanup, so they cannot be reused; paper is shredded. Provider-system deletion follows the provider's policy and exceptions.

6. Device storage, security and rights
Orders, journal entries and imported PDFs use device-lock-protected storage. Automatic cloud sync or restoration after changing devices/reinstalling is not provided. Device data can be deleted in app settings; pending payment/order processing may require completion or refund confirmation first to preserve the order connection.
Operator material has restricted access and is not placed in public repositories. Requests are handled after minimal identity verification; any statutory retention basis and period are explained. Deleting in the app does not automatically delete email-provider, AI-provider or customer-held copies.
Korean privacy complaint channels: KISA privacy report center 118; Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee 1833-6972. Updates and effective dates are published here and in the app, with renewed consent when required. This policy does not limit rights available under applicable law.
