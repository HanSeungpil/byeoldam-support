BYEOLDAM guide

The first page gives a clear recommendation for your concern, followed by the three cards’ reasoning, question-specific answers, a comparison of choices, conditions that would change the recommendation and practical actions. The 78-card deck is shuffled and you choose three cards. Each of the 70 topics has its own illustration. Readings use symbolism and your input; they do not establish future facts or someone else’s thoughts.
